Privacy Policy — PlayKampala

App Name: PlayKampala
Package ID: com.sisilabs.playkampala
Publisher: Ecadel Group Limited / Sisi Labs
Effective Date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PlayKampala handles information when you download, install, and play our mobile or desktop game.

1. No Personal Data Collection

PlayKampala is an offline single-player simulation game. We do not collect, transmit, store on remote servers, sell, or share any personally identifiable information — including your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or advertising identifiers.

2. Local Save Data Only

Game progress — including your chosen rider nickname, home Kampala stage, motorcycle color, outfit, achievements/trophies, and fictional in-game balance — is saved strictly locally on your own device. You may erase this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the game.

3. Fictional In-Game Currency ("MoMo UGX")

All money, fares, fuel refills, repairs, washing bays, and Mobile Money ("MoMo UGX") balances inside PlayKampala are 100% fictional game points. The game does not connect to real mobile money services, banks, or payment processors, and does not request any financial or account credentials.

4. No Third-Party Ads or Tracking SDKs

PlayKampala does not integrate third-party advertising networks, behavioral profiling SDKs, or cross-app tracking tools.

5. Device Audio & System Text-to-Speech

PlayKampala plays bundled local audio files (songs, instrumentals, and sound effects) and may optionally use your device's built-in Text-to-Speech engine for ambient street voices. PlayKampala never accesses your microphone and never records or transmits audio.

6. Children's Privacy

PlayKampala does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or any age group). If you believe any personal information has been provided to us through support channels, contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Future Updates

If future versions of PlayKampala add optional online or multiplayer features that require network connectivity or user accounts, we will update this Privacy Policy and notify players prior to enabling those features.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions or support requests, visit our Support Page or contact the developer email listed on the official Google Play Store listing for PlayKampala.
