PlayKampala — Support & FAQ

How do I ride, refuel, repair, or wash my boda?

  • Move / Steer: Use the bottom-left thumbstick controller (or WASD / Arrow keys on desktop).
  • Mount / Dismount / Interact: Tap the yellow bottom-right action button (or press E / Space).
  • Refuel: Tap any green [F] Petrol Station on the Map, pull up to the pump, and choose your exact amount from UGX 2,000 up to a Full Tank.
  • Repair & Wash: Filter for Repair / Fundi or Wash Bays in the Full Map (M) to plot a GPS route directly there.
  • Customize Rider & Bike: Open Tools → Settings to set your Rider Name, Man/Woman style, Home Stage, bike color, and outfit.

How do I delete my saved data?

PlayKampala stores all save data locally on your device — nothing is stored on external servers. To delete your data, open Android Settings → Apps → PlayKampala → Storage → Clear Storage, or simply uninstall the app.
