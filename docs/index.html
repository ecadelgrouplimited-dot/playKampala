Downtown, Suburbs & Highways

Ride through Kikuubo, Nakasero, Wandegeya, Katwe, Nakivubo Stadium, and Lake Victoria's Ggaba Beach — or head out along Jinja, Gulu, Masaka, and Entebbe highways onto red murram mud roads.

Living Ugandan Traffic

Left-hand traffic filled with blue-and-white checkered matatus, regional buses, 40ft trailers, fellow bodas, KCCA road repairs, traffic police checks, and real roadside confrontations.
