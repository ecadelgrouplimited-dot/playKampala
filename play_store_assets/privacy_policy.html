Privacy Policy — PlayKampala

App Package: com.sisilabs.playkampala
Developer: Sisi Labs
Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Thank you for playing PlayKampala. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how PlayKampala handles information when you install and play the game on Android or desktop devices.

1. No Personal Data Collection

PlayKampala is an offline single-player simulation game. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your real name, email address, phone number, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers) to external servers.

2. Local Save Data Only

Your in-game progress — including your chosen rider nickname, home stage, motorcycle color, outfit, achievements/trophies, and fictional in-game balance — is stored strictly locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes this local save data.

3. Fictional In-Game Currency ("MoMo UGX")

All currency, fares, fuel purchases, vehicle repairs, and Mobile Money ("MoMo UGX") statements inside PlayKampala are 100% fictional gameplay mechanics. PlayKampala does not process real money, banking details, mobile money accounts, or financial transactions.

4. No Third-Party Ads or Analytics SDKs

PlayKampala does not include third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or user-profiling analytics.

5. Device Audio & Text-to-Speech

PlayKampala uses standard local audio playback for in-game music and street sound effects, and optionally invokes the device's built-in system Text-to-Speech engine for ambient street voices. No microphone recording or voice data is collected or transmitted.

6. Children's Privacy

PlayKampala does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If future updates introduce optional online or multiplayer features that require network data, we will update this Privacy Policy prior to releasing those features.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or PlayKampala, please contact Sisi Labs via the developer support email listed on the Google Play Store listing for PlayKampala.
